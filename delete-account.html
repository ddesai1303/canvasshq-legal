Delete Your CanvassHQ Account

Last updated: September 20, 2026

You can request deletion of your CanvassHQ account and personal information at any time, whether you're an organizer, canvasser, or volunteer.

How to request deletion

Email dhavaldesai6991@gmail.com with the subject line "Delete My CanvassHQ Account", sent from the email address associated with your account. Include your name and, if you know it, the campaign(s) you're part of.

What gets deleted

  • Your account profile: name, email address, and login credentials.
  • Any phone number, contact list, or file you personally uploaded that isn't otherwise part of a campaign's shared records.

What may be retained

If you're a canvasser or volunteer, the house visits, sign placements, and tasks you logged are part of the campaign's shared operational history (the organizer relies on this data for reporting and continuity). Deleting your account removes your personal login and profile, but this campaign activity data remains on record, no longer attributed to your name. If you're an organizer and the sole owner of a campaign, let us know in your request whether you'd like that campaign's data deleted as well.

Timeline

We process deletion requests within 30 days of receiving them.
