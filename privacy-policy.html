CanvassHQ Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 19, 2026

This policy describes how CanvassHQ collects and uses information. It is provided for transparency to campaign volunteers, canvassers, and contacts, and to support messaging-compliance registration (e.g. Twilio A2P 10DLC). It is not a substitute for legal advice specific to your campaign's jurisdiction.

CanvassHQ ("the App") is a canvassing and campaign-coordination tool operated by Dhaval Desai ("we", "us", "our"). This policy explains what information the App collects, how it is used, and the choices available to organizers, canvassers, volunteers, and the residents/contacts they interact with.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on how the App is used, we collect:

  • Account information: name, email, and role (organizer, canvasser, or volunteer) for anyone who creates an account.
  • Contact information you or a canvasser enters: a resident or invitee's name, phone number, email address, and home address, when logged during a canvassing visit, sign placement, or event invite.
  • Campaign activity data: house-visit notes, survey answers, interaction type (e.g. supporter, volunteer, donor), sign placement status, RSVP responses, and territory assignments.
  • Location data: approximate latitude/longitude for a logged address, used to place map pins.
  • Technical data: basic crash and error diagnostics (via Sentry) to help us fix bugs. This does not include message content.

2. How We Use Information

  • To operate campaign features: house visit logging, sign placement tracking, territory assignment, surveys, and the volunteer leaderboard.
  • To send event invites, RSVP confirmations, and campaign updates by SMS, WhatsApp, and/or email, to contacts who have given prior consent to be messaged.
  • To let an invitee join a campaign, RSVP to an event, and invite others they know.
  • To diagnose and fix technical problems in the App.

3. Text Messaging & WhatsApp Consent

Phone numbers are only added to the App by an organizer, canvasser, or existing invitee who has already obtained the contact's consent to be messaged — collected verbally in person or via a signed paper sign-up form, before the number is ever entered into CanvassHQ.

  • Message frequency varies based on campaign activity.
  • Message and data rates may apply.
  • Reply STOP at any time to a text message to opt out of future messages.
  • Reply HELP for assistance.
  • Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

4. Who We Share Information With

We share information only as needed to operate the App, with the following service providers:

  • Twilio — to deliver SMS and WhatsApp messages.
  • Brevo — to deliver email messages.
  • Supabase — our database and authentication provider, hosting all app data.
  • Sentry — for crash and error reporting.
  • Google Maps — to render map views of canvassed addresses.

We do not sell personal information, and we do not share phone numbers or message content with third parties for their own marketing purposes.

5. Data Retention

Campaign and contact data is retained for as long as the associated campaign account remains active, or until deletion is requested (see Section 7).

6. Children's Privacy

CanvassHQ is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

7. Your Choices & Rights
